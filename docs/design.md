# Design

Leanings, not decisions yet.

- **Looks like a terminal, feels warm.** Many small panels on one screen, the way btop or a terminal app works. Monospace, keyboard-friendly, no big windows. Small panels stack naturally on small screens. The palette is warm (amber, sepia) rather than cold hacker green, because people write intimate things here.
- **Two voices of type.** Monospace is the frame: the top bar, panel labels, names and dates, buttons. A readable serif carries what people wrote: headlines, stories, forms. That split is the hierarchy, so a first-time visitor sees content first and chrome second.
- **Read first, write second.** One calm column. A visitor sees stories before being asked for one, and can start writing before making an account. Side panels come back only when there is enough to browse.
- **One main action per page.** The home page for a new visitor has one filled button, "tell your story". Everything else is a quiet link.
- **Media is small on purpose.** Images are dithered to a few colors and a few kilobytes, the way [Low-tech Magazine](https://solar.lowtechmagazine.com) does it. That keeps pages fast, storage cheap and faces harder to recognize.
- **No video at the start.** Video costs a lot to store, is hard to moderate and exposes people. We'll revisit it if members ask.
