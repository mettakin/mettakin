# Distribution

The founder will not post daily, and the design accepts that as fact. Reach has to come from the structure itself.

## Mechanisms

1. **Every public experience is a search result.** Each one gets its own page that search engines can read, with a clear title. People search "why do I feel fear when I meditate" every day, and today they find nothing honest. The phenomenon pages gather all those searches.
2. **Citable by AIs.** With open content, clean structure and open data, an AI answering "is it normal to see light in meditation" can cite Mettakin. That's how people find things now.
3. **Authors are the distribution.** Each experience gets a share card: a beautiful quote image plus a link. People share their own story when they want to, never ours.
4. **Automatic feeds, about themes, never about people.** A bot posts about phenomena: "14 meditators have now described fear rising in deep stillness," with a link to that page. Personal stories never get pushed into social feeds. There's also a weekly digest built the same way, and RSS.
5. **Embeds for sanghas and teachers.** A widget shows "experiences from our community" on a retreat center's site. It gives them value and gives us links.
6. **The open build is the story.** A spiritual commons built by humans and AIs together is news. It draws developers and attention once, at launch.
7. **Credit.** Members whose ideas ship get named ([together](together.md)), and people share what they helped make.

## Consent rules

- The author chooses each experience's visibility: **public** (open to the web and search) or **community** (members only). There's no default, so the choice is always conscious.
- Nothing personal leaves Mettakin unless the author sends it themselves.

## What can't be automated

- **The first 50 experiences.** They come from the founder's meditation network, by personal invitation.
- **One honest launch post** in places where meditators already talk, like r/streamentry or r/Meditation. It's one real ask for first experiences, not a campaign.
- **The first retreat center or teacher** who invites their people in.

After that, the mechanisms above carry the reach.
